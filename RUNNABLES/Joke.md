# JOKES

A caveman brought a primitive AI tool to his tribe to help them hunt. He asked the AI for the best way to catch a mammoth. The AI spent three days calculating the physics of spears and wind resistance before suggesting they try to lure the mammoth into a giant charging port. The tribe decided to just keep hitting the mammoth with rocks instead.

# EXPLANATION

The humor lies in the contrast between advanced technology and primal reality. The AI is programmed to provide logical, data-driven solutions based on its own existence, leading it to suggest a charging port—an object useless to cavemen. The joke mocks how AI often provides technically sophisticated but contextually irrelevant or absurd answers. By ignoring the complex, impractical advice and choosing the simple, effective method of throwing rocks, the cavemen highlight the superiority of common sense over disconnected, high-tech logic in practical survival situations.
